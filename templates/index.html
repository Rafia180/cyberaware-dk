Learn to spot a scam before it costs you

Scammers copy MitID, your bank, your landlord and Danish authorities. This site shows you what those messages look like and what to do about them. Every example is invented for learning.

Learn about scams Get help now

Three steps that work for every scam

  1. Stop

    Do not click, pay or approve anything yet. Nothing genuine falls apart because you waited ten minutes.

  2. Check

    Open the organisation's own app or website yourself. Never use the link or phone number you were sent.

  3. Ask

    Tell a friend, classmate or your university. Scammers count on you deciding alone and quickly.
