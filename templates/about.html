About this project

CyberAware DK is an educational student project, submitted as part of a BSc Computer Science final year project.

What it is for

It helps international students in Denmark recognise common social engineering scams. Social engineering means tricking a person rather than breaking into a computer system, and it covers fake emails, text messages and calls, but also fraudulent rental adverts, fake job offers and impersonation of Danish authorities.

The content is built around Danish services by name, including MitID, SKAT, Digital Post and the Immigration Service, because that is what the most convincing scams in Denmark imitate.

What it does not do

It is a learning resource, not a scam detector. It does not check whether a message you received is genuine, and it never asks for your information. It does not access your email, text messages, passwords, bank accounts, CPR number, MitID or any other private data.

This is a deliberate decision rather than a missing feature. An automatic verdict on a real message could be wrong, and being wrongly told a message is safe is more dangerous than having no tool at all.

Where the content comes from

Every example message is invented for teaching. None was copied from a real victim or a real scam. Each topic reflects patterns described in official Danish guidance, and each page links to the sources it is based on so you can read them yourself.
